# v18 world2 camera

Untested. Same island as v17 except the saddle height sign is flipped: `y = 5 * (z²/900 − x²/1600)`. East-west rims are now the low sides, north-south rims the high sides. Footprints are unchanged. The potato mesh is half of v17 (width 0.5). Collision radii are still the v17 values.

Normal play: WASD moves relative to the camera heading. Arrow keys and the right mouse button change yaw and pitch. 重置视角 restores the default follow angle around the current potato. Zoom stays in 取景模式 only.
